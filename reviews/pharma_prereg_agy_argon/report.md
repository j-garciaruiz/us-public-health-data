## Ranked Method and Factual Findings (with Concrete Fixes)

### 1. [P1 Falsifiability Defect] The `$245` semaglutide carve-out is not applied to P1's aggregate `90%` unweighted and `97%` claim-weighted pass/fail bands
- **Issue:** [prereg_2027.md](file:///Users/hari/Documents/VSC%20Python/US%20Data/.claude/worktrees/pharma-data-eae972/pharma/prereg_2027.md#L19-L22) requires (Bullet 2) at least `90%` of plan-NDC rows and `97%` claim-weighted to fall *within* `0.5%` of the MFP (`[0.995, 1.005]`), while Bullet 5 says that if semaglutide's median ratio is `< 0.95` (due to the `$245` MFN agreement, where `$245/$274 = 0.894`), that is "not a failure of the MFP to bind." However, semaglutide accounts for **45.4% of all 2026 Q1 cohort claims** (`3.36M / 7.40M`) and **12.1% of 30-day SPUF rows** (`33,030 / 273,546`). If semaglutide prices at `$245` (`ratio ~ 0.894`), the cohort share within `[0.995, 1.005]` mechanically falls to at most `54.6%` claim-weighted and `87.9%` unweighted, causing P1 Bullet 2 to fail even if all 15 drugs bind at or below the MFP.
- **Concrete Fix:** Amend P1 Bullet 2 to either (a) test the share at or below `1.005` (`ratio <= 1.005`) alongside `>= 90%` in `[0.995, 1.005]` among drugs without a sub-MFP MFN price, or (b) explicitly state that if P1 Bullet 5 (`semaglutide median < 0.95`) triggers, Bullet 2's `90%` and `97%` within-`0.5%` bands are scored on the remaining 14 drugs.

### 2. [P2 Threshold Defect] The unconditional Spearman $\rho \ge 0.80$ rule across all 15 drugs fails on the 2026 calibration cohort itself ($\rho = 0.7212$)
- **Issue:** [prereg_2027.md](file:///Users/hari/Documents/VSC%20Python/US%20Data/.claude/worktrees/pharma-data-eae972/pharma/prereg_2027.md#L49-L51) excludes drugs with pre-existing generic entry or list-price cuts from the per-drug accuracy band (`+/- 4 pp`), but states the rank-order criterion (`Spearman >= 0.8`) across *all 15 drugs* without that exclusion. On the 10-drug 2026 calibration cohort in [`calibration_2026.csv`](file:///Users/hari/Documents/VSC%20Python/US%20Data/.claude/worktrees/pharma-data-eae972/pharma/outputs/prereg_2027/calibration_2026.csv), the Spearman rank correlation across all 10 drugs is **0.7212** (which fails `0.80`), whereas across the 8 non-structural drugs it is **0.9762**.
- **Concrete Fix:** Apply the same structural exclusion (drugs without small-molecule generic launch by `2027 Q1` or list-price cut before `2026-10-01`) to the Spearman $\rho \ge 0.80$ test in P2, or lower the all-15-drug Spearman threshold to `0.70`.

### 3. [P2 Baseline & Unrecorded Risk] Mismatch between October 2026 SPUF ($r$) and full-year 2026 Part D baseline, compounded by mid-2026 price jumps (`Xifaxan`)
- **Issue:** [prereg_2027.md](file:///Users/hari/Documents/VSC%20Python/US%20Data/.claude/worktrees/pharma-data-eae972/pharma/prereg_2027.md#L24-L28) defines P2's realized outcome as `2026 (full year)` to `2027 Q1`, while $r$ is measured in the `2026-10-01` SPUF and [`prereg_2027.py`](file:///Users/hari/Documents/VSC%20Python/US%20Data/.claude/worktrees/pharma-data-eae972/pharma/scripts/prereg_2027.py#L139-L141) computes `pc_2027Q1_pred` and the `-63.4%` fixed-mix drop off `2026 Q1` Part D spending. Moreover, P2 line 27 says the band is widened to `4 pp` *"because the 2026 baseline is a full year that includes January 2026 list-price changes."* A `2026-01-01` cut (such as Linzess's `-50.3%` cut) applies to all four quarters of 2026 and does not create a gap between full-year 2026 and October 2026. What *does* create a gap is a mid-year 2026 price change: in [`spuf_summary_by_drug.csv`](file:///Users/hari/Documents/VSC%20Python/US%20Data/.claude/worktrees/pharma-data-eae972/pharma/outputs/spuf/spuf_summary_by_drug.csv), `Xifaxan`'s SPUF ratio jumped **+16.0%** between January 2026 (`2.803`) and October 2026 (`3.253`), which will make $1/r_{\text{Oct 2026}} - 1$ (`-69.3%`) overpredict the drop relative to full-year 2026 Part D spending.
