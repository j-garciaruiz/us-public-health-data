# Independent source audit, 2026-10-20

**Verdict: PASS WITH FIXES.** All 20 explicitly linked DOIs identify real publications matching the cited authors and subjects. Most numerical summaries and the main CMS dates are supported. However, the gross-to-net arithmetic is wrong, the explanation attributed to Wouters is wrong, the 2027 CMS comparison year is misstated, and the novelty claims need substantial narrowing. These corrections are necessary before relying on the synthesis or circulating it as a verified review.

Scope: `literature/ira_negotiation_literature_review.md` and only the “What others have found (context)” section of `pharma/ira_findings.md`. References below use **L** for line numbers in the literature review and **F** for line numbers in the findings file. The underlying analysis, charts, and other findings sections were not audited. Author working notes in `literature/ira_notes/` were not used as evidence. No source files were edited and no commit was made.

Each linked DOI was attempted directly and checked against accessible publisher content, PubMed/Europe PMC, Crossref, or an author manuscript. A DOI resolving establishes identity, not the correctness of every claim. **CONFIRMED** means the stated claim is supported at the access level identified; **WRONG** means a source or reproducible calculation contradicts the wording; **COULD NOT CHECK** means access, identification, or evidentiary limits prevented verification. Blocked pages are not treated as false. Repeated claims are grouped, with all relevant locations identified. Additional checks cover named but unlinked sources and the synthesis claims that depend on them.
