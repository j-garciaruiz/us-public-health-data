## 1. Plan Year Identification Across SPUF Releases

Although two of the three CMS quarterly SPUF folders carry `2025` in their directory and inner-file labels (`2025Q3` and `2025Q4`), the underlying data belong to **Plan Year 2025** for `2025Q3` and **Plan Year 2026** for both `2025Q4` and `2026Q3`:

1. **Eliquis 5 mg (`00003089421`) unit cost:** In `2025Q3`, the 30-day median unit cost is `$10.0521` (2.4251 times the 2026 MFP of `$4.145072`). In `2025Q4` (compiled 2026-01-05, released 2026-01-07), the median unit cost drops immediately to `$4.1451` (`$4.145072` rounded to four decimal places, ratio `1.000007`), matching `2026Q3`.
2. **Plan Information metadata:** `plan information PPUF_2025Q3.txt` uses CY 2025 formulary IDs (`00025...`) and the CY 2025 maximum standard deductible (`$590`). Both `plan information PPUF_2025Q4.txt` and `plan information PPUF_2026Q3.txt` use CY 2026 formulary IDs (`00026...`) and the CY 2026 maximum deductible (`$615`).

## 2. MFP Mapping and Filtering Rules

From `data/raw/pharma/ira/selected_drugs_mfp.csv`:
- **Retroactively voided / deselected rows (`MFP End Date < MFP Effective Date`):**
  - 59 rows for IPAY 2026 drugs deselected after 2026 have `MFP Effective Date = 01/01/2027` and `MFP End Date = 12/31/2026`.
  - 4 Xarelto NDCs (`50458-0577-01`, `50458-0578-01`, `50458-0579-01`, `50458-0580-01`) have duplicate `01/01/2026` rows where one row was end-dated `12/31/2025` for a package-size update and replaced by a valid `01/01/2026` to `12/31/2026` row with the same unit price.
  - 7 repackager NDCs (4 Xarelto `55154-*` and 3 Entresto `71610-*`) have `MFP Effective Date = 01/01/2026` and `MFP End Date = 12/31/2025` ("NDC removed from NDC list and end dated to 12/31/2025"); none of these 7 NDCs appear in any of the three SPUF pricing files.
  - Filtering to valid periods (`MFP End Date >= MFP Effective Date` or blank `MFP End Date`) yields **144 unique valid NDC-11s** across the 10 IPAY 2026 drugs and **160 unique valid NDC-11s** across the 15 IPAY 2027 drugs.
- **Time-specific MFP for IPAY 2026:**
  - `2025Q3` (Plan Year 2025 baseline): compared against the 2026 MFP using the corrected post-2026-06-01 MFP for Entresto Sprinkle (`$5.521349` for `00078-1231-20` and `$5.648068` for `00078-1238-20`).
  - `2025Q4` (Plan Year 2026, Jan 2026 release): compared against the MFP in effect in January 2026 (`01/01/2026` to `05/31/2026`), where Entresto Sprinkle's imputed unit MFP was `$1.229167` for both `00078-1231-20` and `00078-1238-20` (and plans in `2025Q4` priced both at `$1.2292` median).
  - `2026Q3` (Plan Year 2026, Oct 2026 release): compared against the MFP in effect from `06/01/2026` to `12/31/2026`, where Entresto Sprinkle's recalculated unit MFP is `$5.521349` (`00078-1231-20`) and `$5.648068` (`00078-1238-20`) (and plans in `2026Q3` priced them at `$5.5213` and `$5.6481` median).
- **MFP for IPAY 2027:**
  - All three releases are compared against the negotiated `01/01/2027` MFP per unit (not yet in effect during 2025 or 2026).
- **Plan count definitions:**
  - In SPUF, the unique plan-segment key is `(CONTRACT_ID, PLAN_ID, SEGMENT_ID)` (`Plans (Seg)` below), which has zero duplicate `(plan, NDC)` rows at `DAYS_SUPPLY == 30`. We also report unique `(CONTRACT_ID, PLAN_ID)` (`Plans (No Seg)`).
- **Tolerance bands:**
  - Within 0.5% of MFP: `0.995 <= Ratio <= 1.005` (`|Ratio - 1| <= 0.005`).
  - Below MFP: `Ratio < 0.995`.
  - Above MFP: `Ratio > 1.005`.
