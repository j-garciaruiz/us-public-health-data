# Part D plan prices vs the Maximum Fair Price

Generated by `pharma/scripts/spuf_mfp_check.py`. Ratio = plan unit cost (30-day supply) / MFP per unit. For IPAY 2026 drugs the MFP is in effect from 2026-01-01; for IPAY 2027 drugs it starts 2027-01-01, so their 2026 ratios measure the gap to the future 2027 MFP. Counts are plan-segment x NDC pairs ("plans" are contract-plan-segments), unweighted; "at the MFP" = within 0.5%. UNIT_COST is the plan's reported average retail unit price for the NDC, in NCPDP billing units (EA/ML/GM) like the MFP per unit; it excludes dispensing fees (reported separately) and includes concessions applied at the point of sale, but shows no rebate amounts, net plan spending or patient cost sharing. SPUF lists one proxy NDC per drug concept, so only some of the CMS list's NDCs appear, and the proxy can change between releases.
