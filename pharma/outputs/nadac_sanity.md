# NADAC 2026: sanity summary

Generated by `pharma/scripts/summarize_nadac.py` from `data/raw/pharma/nadac/nadac_2026.csv` and `nadac_2025.csv`.
NADAC is the CMS survey estimate of the average invoice price retail community pharmacies pay, per pricing unit (EA = tablet/capsule/each, ML, GM). Generic rates come from the monthly survey; brand rates also follow WAC changes between surveys. It excludes rebates paid to payers and off-invoice discounts to pharmacies.
