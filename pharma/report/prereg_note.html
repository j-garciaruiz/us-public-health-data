Amendments after review

Amendment 1 (2026-10-20, after the Codex review). Codex reproduced all 221 baseline numbers. The amendment fixes how each prediction is scored without changing any registered number: every clause is scored separately as pass, fail or not evaluable, with no overall verdict; the primary plan-price test uses the first CMS release containing plan-year 2027 unit prices, expected in January 2027, with later releases as replications; rows are contract x plan x segment x NDC with a 30-day supply, at full precision against the frozen 2026-10-20 MFP file after dropping superseded rows; missing products are listed, never counted as passes. P2 fixes each drug's weight at its 2026 Q1 claims: the observed index is the sum of 2026 Q1 claims times 2027 Q1 spending per claim over the sum of 2026 Q1 claims times full-year 2026 spending per claim, minus 1, scored against the registered −68% to −58%. The semaglutide exception is withdrawn: its median ratio must lie between 0.995 and 1.005, inclusive, like every other 2027 drug; compliance with the MFP ceiling is reported separately (Medicare's $50-copay GLP-1 Bridge operates outside Part D coverage and payment through 2027). It also corrects the registration's data note: the monthly 2027 release CMS posted on 2026-10-08 has no pricing table, so the plan-price test waits for the January 2027 quarterly release. The calibration is reported in full: across all ten drugs the mean absolute error is 4.7 points and the rank correlation 0.72, so P2's rank clause (0.8) is a harder test than the rule has passed so far.

Amendment 2 (2026-10-20, after the Agy review). Each Agy finding was checked against the data before use. Applied: the missing Fiasp row was restored to one baseline table and Farxiga's 2026 MFP corrected to $178.50. Five sensitivities were specified: a Q4-only 2026 baseline for all 15 drugs (Q4 year-to-date minus Q3 year-to-date spending and claims); separate Ozempic (r = 3.66) and Wegovy (r = 3.33) results, keeping the pooled semaglutide prediction primary; brand-only plan-price shares, keeping every listed NDC against its own MFP in P1; a 2026 Q1 baseline for P4 and P5; and a 2028 comparator excluding drugs with WAC-based cuts of at least 20% effective 2026-12-20 through 2027-02-28. Xifaxan's ratio rose from 2.80 to 3.25 during 2026, so its decline against the full-year baseline is expected to be smaller than the registered October-based −69%. Trulicity is 42% and Anoro Ellipta 13% of the 2028 comparator's 2026 Q1 claims. Not applied: Agy listed wrong values for Gabriel et al.'s discounts while calling them an exact match, described a scoring band that does not exist, and summarized a paper with figures not in its text. The rank-correlation threshold stays at 0.8.
