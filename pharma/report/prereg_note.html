Amendments after review

Amendment 1 (2026-10-20, after the Codex review). Codex reproduced all 221 baseline numbers. The amendment fixes how each prediction is scored without changing any registered number: every clause is scored separately as pass, fail or not evaluable, with no overall verdict; the unit is plan x segment x product rows with a 30-day supply against the MFP file as downloaded on 2026-10-20; missing products are listed, never counted as passes. The semaglutide escape clause is withdrawn, so a semaglutide median below the MFP is a fail of P1 (Medicare's $50-copay GLP-1 Bridge runs outside Part D through 2027). It also corrects the registration's data note: the monthly 2027 release CMS posted on 2026-10-08 has no pricing table, so the plan-price test waits for the January 2027 quarterly release. The calibration is reported in full: across all ten drugs the mean absolute error is 4.7 points and the rank correlation 0.72, so P2's rank clause (0.8) is a harder test than the rule has passed so far.

Amendment 2 (2026-10-20, after the Agy review). Each Agy finding was checked against the data before use. Applied: a code fix (the Fiasp row was dropped from one baseline table) and five pre-specified sensitivities: a 2026 Q4 baseline (Xifaxan's plan price rose 16% during 2026, so a full-year baseline will understate its fall), semaglutide by brand (Ozempic r = 3.66, Wegovy r = 3.33), brand-only plan prices, a 2026 Q1 baseline for the drugs leaving and continuing, and a 2028 comparator without drugs that cut list prices (Trulicity is 42% of its claims). Not applied: Agy listed wrong values for Gabriel et al.'s discounts while calling them an exact match, described a scoring band that does not exist, and summarized a paper with figures not in its text. The rank-correlation threshold stays at 0.8.
