About your point that P-B-Ms hike prices: it is the manufacturers who set list prices. The criticism of P-B-Ms is that their pay has been tied to high list prices and big rebates, which rewards everyone for keeping sticker prices high. A related problem: one study found that in early twenty twenty-one, Part D plans paid pharmacies on average five point four times what the top fifty generics cost them, though it could not see who kept the money after fees. Congress reacted this year: from twenty twenty-eight, P-B-Ms in Medicare can only earn flat fees not linked to drug prices, and from twenty twenty-nine they must pass all rebates to employer plans.
