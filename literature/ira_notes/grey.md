# Grey literature scan: Medicare Drug Price Negotiation Program (IRA, 2022)

Scoping scan for `pharma/ira_findings.md`. Compiled 2026-10-20.

## How searched

On 2026-10-20 I ran web searches in standard mode (extended mode for thin topics: Q1 2026 spending monitoring, pharmacy losses, list-price cuts by 46brooklyn, AbbVie litigation, EPIC Act status) and then opened every item listed in the tables below, either with a web fetch or by downloading the PDF and extracting its text (`pdftotext`). Federal Register documents were checked through the federalregister.gov API (titles, dates, abstracts). An item counts as verified only if I opened the page or document and saw its date and the numbers quoted here; anything seen only in search snippets or secondary reporting is listed under "Not verified" at the end. Several sites refused automated access (HTTP 403 or login redirect): cbo.gov, congress.gov, healthaffairs.org, fiercehealthcare.com, fiercepharma.com, medrxiv.org, link.springer.com, thinkbrg.com, ascp.com, beckershospitalreview.com, commonwealthfund.org (one brief). For CBO, numbers are reported through verified secondary sources and flagged as such. Advocacy and trade-group sources are labeled by type; their numbers are their own claims. Numbers are quoted as the source states them; I did not recompute them.

Relevance key for the last column: F1 = our finding 1 (Part D gross spending per claim fell, median -64%); F2 = finding 2 (WAC cuts on 2026-01-01 and later); F3 = finding 3 (NADAC 1.3 to 3.6 times MFP; pharmacy refund exposure); L = limits section (gross vs net, mix, causality).
